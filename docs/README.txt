Lim — tidy up your Downloads folder

Install
1. Unzip the folder wherever you like (for example in Documents).
2. Double-click "Lim.exe".
3. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (the exe is not code-signed yet):
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The app opens in its own window. Click "Scan".
   Closing the window keeps the app next to the clock: click the icon to open it again.

On first launch, the exe adds a "Lim" shortcut to the Start menu and starts with
Windows (icon next to the clock only). To turn that off: right-click the icon.

Good to know
- Nothing is deleted right away: everything spends 30 days in quarantine, and every action can be undone.
- Auto-sort is off at first: the "Set up auto-sort" button at the top configures it.
- Language: FR / EN button at the top right (the app follows your Windows language at first).
- Lim, the little robot, shows you around on first launch; its head at the top right brings it back.
- Your data (history, quarantine) lives in %LOCALAPPDATA%\DownloadsManager.

Don't move the exe after the first launch: the shortcuts point to where it is.
(If you do move it, run it once from its new place: it updates the shortcuts.)
